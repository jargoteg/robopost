## 1. Topic Performance
**Over-performed:** Humanoid navigation (12 likes, 3 reposts) and wheel-legged/hybrid locomotion topics consistently outperform. Human-robot interaction framing (juggling, football) attracts replies even when likes are low. **Under-performed:** Highly technical single-system papers (tendon hysteresis, wrench control, EKF estimation) near-universally scored zero across all metrics. Surgical/medical robotics showed mild interest (2 likes) but no amplification.

## 2. Hook Styles That Worked
**bold_claim** drove the top post by a wide margin. **curiosity_gap** produced the most consistent reply generation (engagement signal even at low scale). **number_stat** and **tension** rarely broke through.
