**Bold claim** is the clear winner — "Zero robot data" drove the top post; "Football reveals what balance controllers can't fake" earned 3 likes. It creates immediate stakes.

**Curiosity gap** produced modest but consistent replies (surgical robots, legged manipulation). **Number stat** and **tension** hooks underperformed despite frequency of use.

## 4. Three Concrete Recommendations

1. **Lead with humanoids and human-robot interaction.** This is your only proven viral category. Prioritize papers where a robot operates near, with, or like a human.

2. **Retire number_stat as a primary hook.** Specific percentages ("33.3% better," "31-33% more wrench error") fail to create emotional pull. Reserve for slide body copy only.

3. **Test bold_claim on mid-tier topics.** Your top performer was bold_claim + humanoid. Before concluding topics drive everything, run bold_claim hooks on 3 non-humanoid papers to isolate whether hook or topic is doing the work.
