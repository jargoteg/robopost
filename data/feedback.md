## 4. Concrete Recommendations

2. **Reframe number_stat hooks narratively** — "33.3% better peg insertion" means nothing cold; wrap the stat inside a bold_claim frame first.
3. **Pre-screen papers for figure availability before briefing writers** — rejected figures are wasting pipeline capacity; add this as step one in vetting.
