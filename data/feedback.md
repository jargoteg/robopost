# Content Team — Lessons Learned Brief

## 1. Topic Performance
**Over-performed:** Visually surprising mechanics (SAWbot wave propulsion, wheel-legged football, 32-legged amoeba, juggling robot) and multi-robot coordination (congestion-aware planning). The "weird robot does unexpected thing" angle consistently pulled reactions.
**Under-performed:** Highly technical single-metric papers (tendon hysteresis, orbital wrench control, swarm occlusion transport) and anything medical/sensor-heavy without a clear visual hook.

## 2. Hook Styles That Worked
**Bold_claim** and **curiosity_gap** drove the highest single-post like counts (2–3 likes). **Number_stat** earned the only repost in the dataset (congestion planner). **Tension** reliably generated replies but rarely likes — it opens conversation without signaling shareability.

## 3. Carousel vs. Video

## 4. Three Concrete Recommendations
1. **Lead with robot behavior, not robot specs.** "Wheel-legged robots play football" outperformed "soft hand passes Kapandji test" — frame around *what it does*, not *what it measures*.
2. **Pair number_stat hooks with multi-robot or task-completion papers** — the only repost came from this combination. Prioritize papers with a single surprising benchmark number in the abstract.
3. **Data is too sparse for strong conclusions** — 30 posts, all near-zero engagement. Before optimizing hooks, test posting time, use of alt-text/image quality, and whether tagging paper authors drives initial seeding. Run 10 more posts before re-weighting.
