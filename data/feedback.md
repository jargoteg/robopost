# Content Team: Lessons Learned Brief

## 1. Topic Performance
**Over-performed:** Humanoid robots (navigation from human data: 16 total engagements), wheel-legged/hybrid locomotion, and multi-robot coordination. Relatable, visual, "wow" robotics wins.
**Under-performed:** Niche sensor/control papers (hysteresis, wrench control, EKF), underwater/infrastructure robots, and anything without striking visuals (consistent rejection pattern).

## 2. Hook Styles That Worked
`bold_claim` drove the two highest-performing posts (humanoid navigation, wheel-legged football). `curiosity_gap` showed modest consistency. `number_stat` and `tension` rarely broke above 1–2 likes.

## 4. Three Concrete Recommendations
1. **Lead with humanoid and hybrid locomotion papers** — they consistently attract the highest engagement
2. **Default to `bold_claim` hooks**; retire `tension` unless the paper has a genuinely striking tradeoff
3. **Hard-filter papers without usable figures before writing** — rejection rate is high and wastes production effort
