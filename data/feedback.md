**Over-performed:** Humanoid robotics (navigation from human data: 16 total engagements), wheel-legged robots (3 likes), and multi-robot coordination. Human-relatable narratives ("fits your hallway," "walks through crowds") consistently outperformed pure technical papers.
**Under-performed:** Niche hardware papers (tendon hysteresis, wrench control, spinning drones) and anything without strong visual figures — the rejection queue confirms figures are a hard gate.
