## 1. Topic Performance
**Over-performed:** Humanoid robots (navigation from human data: 16 total interactions), wheel-legged/hybrid locomotion, and multi-robot coordination. Human-interest framing (juggling, football) drives replies even at low like counts. **Under-performed:** Highly technical sensor/control papers (hysteresis, wrench control, tendon systems) — likely too niche without a visceral hook.

## 2. Hook Styles That Worked
`bold_claim` generated the highest single post (11 likes, humanoid nav). `curiosity_gap` produced consistent replies (surgical robots, legged manipulation). `number_stat` underperformed expectations — the numbers chosen were often too abstract to feel surprising.

## 4. Three Concrete Recommendations
1. **Lead with human stakes** — the humanoid walking post succeeded because the hook implied real-world impact, not technical novelty.
2. **Retire weak number_stat hooks** unless the number is genuinely counterintuitive (avoid "33.3% better").
3. **Test one crosspost to Instagram/TikTok** — all metrics there are zero, making it impossible to optimize channel mix.
