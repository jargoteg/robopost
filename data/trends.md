# Community trends (updated 2026-09-25T21:09Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several fronts right now. Humanoid robots are dominating conversation, with heavy skepticism about demo authenticity, corporate hype, and whether the humanoid form factor even makes sense. Russia's collapsing debut robot and the mocap-suit-as-robot meme are getting significant traction. Wealth and AI/robotics as a poverty solution is generating heated pushback, likely tied to the viral billionaire post. Realbotix's pivot from sex robots to something else is sparking a detailed thread from a researcher that people are engaging with seriously. On the engineering side, there's genuine enthusiasm for mechanical fundamentals like the Oldham coupling and folded structures in robotics design. Serve Robotics and Unitree are appearing in scattered product conversations. Swarm robotics is a quieter but growing thread.

For a post that lands today, lean into the humanoid skepticism wave with a sharp take on why non-humanoid designs consistently outperform humanoid ones in real deployments. The audience is primed for it.
