# Community trends (updated 2026-09-08T20:47Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several fronts right now. Humanoid robots are dominating conversation, driven by the Chinese robot beating Usain Bolt's 100m record at the World Humanoid Robot Games in Beijing, Russia's robot collapsing at its debut, and ongoing skepticism about whether humanoid form factors make any sense at all. AI and robotics as a response to poverty is generating heated pushback, particularly targeting billionaire techno-optimism. OpenAI's Pentagon robotics deal and undefined guardrails around military AI are drawing serious concern from researchers. The Realbotix pivot from sex robots to classroom companions is sparking a lively thread from a robotics researcher. Classic engineering content is performing surprisingly well, with the Oldham coupling and folded-structure posts going wide. Swarm robotics is a quieter but growing undercurrent among academics.

For a post angle today, lean into the Beijing humanoid games controversy and ask whether speed benchmarks actually tell us anything meaningful about robot utility.
