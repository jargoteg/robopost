# Community trends (updated 2026-08-08T06:52Z)

The robotics community on Bluesky is buzzing across several distinct conversations right now. Humanoid robots dominate engagement, with heavy skepticism about their practicality, authenticity of demos, and whether they represent genuine vision or marketing theater. There's notable viral mockery of both Russian and American humanoid robot failures. A parallel debate about AI and robotics as solutions to poverty is drawing sharp critical responses to billionaire techno-optimism. Robot learning data is quietly gaining traction, particularly around the underuse of failure clips from robot labs. Swarm robotics is generating quieter but genuine intellectual interest, contrasting with the humanoid hype. The Realbotix pivot from sex robots to classroom robots is sparking a substantive thread from an actual researcher worth amplifying. 

For a post angle that would land today: lean into the failure-clip robot learning angle. Frame it around why the robotics field's most valuable dataset is the one everyone deletes, and invite researchers to share what they've thrown away.
