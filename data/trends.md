# Community trends (updated 2026-09-24T03:24Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several distinct conversations right now. Humanoid robots dominate engagement, with heavy skepticism about industry hype, authenticity of demos, and a viral moment around Russia's collapsing "Aidol" robot. There's sharp tension between excitement and cynicism about billionaire-backed claims that AI and robotics will solve poverty. The Realbotix pivot from sex robots to something new is generating genuine researcher-led discussion worth watching. On the technical side, swarm robotics is a quieter but growing thread, with ants and decentralized systems framed as a smarter alternative to humanoid obsession. Serve Robotics and Unitree are getting named specifically in real-world deployment chatter. The Oldham coupling and folded-structure posts show appetite for classic engineering content that reframes robotics history.

Suggested post angle: lean into the humanoid skepticism wave with a straightforward question asking what non-humanoid robot form factor people think is actually most useful in the real world right now. That framing will generate strong replies today.
