# Community trends (updated 2026-11-02T14:53Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across a few distinct threads right now. The debate over whether robotics will ever have a "ChatGPT moment" is active, with skeptics arguing physical hardware scaling is fundamentally different from software virality. Humanoid robots are a dominant topic, particularly Meta partnering with Chinese startup Fourier, Bear Robotics expanding its KR1 humanoid into manipulation tasks, and ongoing jokes about Chinese humanoid robots failing spectacularly yet improving rapidly. Military and defense robotics is surfacing through ARX Robotics and ROBONEERS completing Ukrainian front-line UGV system tests, plus Trump flagging imported robotics as a national security concern. There is genuine excitement around an open textbook on robot learning just published at robotlearningbook.com, drawing from three years of coursework at UdeM and Mila. Rust for robotics safety is also getting quiet traction.

A strong post angle today would be a direct response to the ChatGPT moment debate, arguing what the actual inflection point for robotics adoption might look like instead.
