# Community trends (updated 2026-08-04T08:36Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several distinct conversations right now. The biggest tension is around humanoid robots, with heavy skepticism dominating: people are mocking rushed demos (Russia's collapsing Aidol robot is getting roasted), questioning the vision behind humanoid form factors, and pushing back on AI/robotics as a poverty solution from billionaires. Realbotix's pivot from sex robots to classrooms is generating a serious thread from a robotics researcher and learning scientist worth watching. The classroom robot deployment pause in upstate New York is fueling debate about AI in education. On the technical side, folded structures for force distribution and the Oldham coupling's robotics relevance are both getting strong engagement, suggesting appetite for engineering history and mechanics content. Swarm robotics is a quieter but emerging thread. OpenAI's robotics team raising guardrails concerns about the Pentagon deal is drawing attention.

Post angle that would land today: a sharp, evidence-based take on why swarm robotics better solves real-world problems than humanoid form factors, riding the anti-humanoid skepticism wave.
