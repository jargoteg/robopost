# Community trends (updated 2026-11-17T15:34Z)

The robotics community is buzzing around several converging stories right now. The dominant conversation is the collapse of Foundation Future Industries, the Eric Trump-backed humanoid robot startup that missed payroll while allegedly pursuing Pentagon and Israeli military contracts — generating both outrage and dark humor. Closely tied to this is broader skepticism about the humanoid robot form factor itself, with many voices arguing bipedal designs are vanity engineering with no practical advantage. OpenAI quietly shutting down its robotics division is fueling a wider "AI collapse" narrative, with people questioning whether general-purpose AI is anywhere near ready for embodied systems. The Robotics and AI ETF hitting June 2025 lows is adding financial anxiety to the mix. On the lighter side, robot wars content and OC robot characters are generating warm engagement. CoRL 2026's Physical AI Safety workshop is the one event getting traction in research circles.
