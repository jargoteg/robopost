# Community trends (updated 2026-07-29T08:41Z)

The robotics community on Bluesky right now is split across a few distinct conversations. Humanoid robots dominate engagement, driven largely by skepticism and mockery: Russia's collapsing "Aidol" debut, the New York high school robot teacher controversy, and ongoing jokes about "autonomous" demos secretly being remote-controlled are all drawing heavy interaction. A viral mocap-suit-kicks-himself clip is the top post overall. Separately, there's a low-key but active swarm robotics thread with researchers sharing papers, the SWARM Biotactics bioelectronic insect story, and new work on self-organizing robot hierarchies. Engineering curiosity posts about the Oldham coupling and folded structures are punching well above their weight in engagement. The wealth-and-AI-solving-poverty discourse is simmering with clear frustration. Language preservation through robotics (the Michigan 12-year-old story) is a rare feel-good thread getting traction.

Suggested post angle: riff on the humanoid robot skepticism wave with something concrete, like cataloging the gap between press releases and actual demos this year. That tension is clearly where the community wants to vent.
