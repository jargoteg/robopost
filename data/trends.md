# Community trends (updated 2026-11-26T01:37Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several fronts today. Foundation Future Industries, the Eric Trump-backed humanoid robotics company, is generating significant negative attention after employees lost health insurance without warning, signaling financial distress. Autonomous manufacturing is gaining traction, with Foundry Robotics' $100M raise and broader funding projections nearly tripling to $60.7B for 2026 dominating investment conversation. Military and defense robotics remains hot, with Ukrainian UGV company RATEL, German Quantum Systems' €10M+ Ukraine commitment, and battlefield humanoid robots all drawing engagement. The ethics and regulation of killer/autonomous robots is sparking heated debate, particularly around data collection practices. Evolutionary robotics and legged robot navigation (the ROAM hybrid mobility system) are generating quieter but genuine technical enthusiasm.
