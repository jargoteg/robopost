# Community trends (updated 2026-10-27T17:36Z)

The dominant conversation right now is the militarization of robotics and the ethical trap facing engineers who want to build civilian tech but find defense funding is the only path available. This is generating real frustration and soul-searching, with several posts capturing the "I thought we were making dancing robots" disillusionment. Closely related is a thread about DARPA and SBIR grants having quietly shaped the entire modern robotics industry, which reframes that ethical problem historically. A secondary theme is humanoid robots, touching on Toyota's push against Tesla and China, a reported humanoid robot worker strike, and skepticism about Elon Musk's claims. The robot learning open textbook from a UdeM/Mila researcher is getting quiet but genuine traction. Asimov's Three Laws are being invoked with knowing irony.

Suggested post angle: address the young person whose parent posted about their 16-year-old wanting a robotics career. What does an honest, non-weapons path actually look like in 2026? That tension is live and unresolved.
