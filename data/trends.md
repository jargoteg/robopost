# Community trends (updated 2026-08-10T18:57Z)

The robotics community on Bluesky is buzzing across several distinct conversations right now. Humanoid robots dominate discussion, mostly skeptically, with people mocking failed demos, questioning their practicality, and dunking on Musk's Optimus promises. There's a parallel thread about AI and robotics as solutions to societal problems like poverty, met with heavy cynicism toward billionaire techno-optimism. Robot learning is generating genuine technical interest, particularly a notable demo of a humanoid learning crowd navigation from just five hours of human walking footage with no robot training data. The Realbotix pivot from sex robots to classroom companions is sparking a surprisingly substantive thread from a robotics researcher. Classic engineering content is also performing well, with the Oldham coupling and origami-inspired force distribution posts racking up thousands of engagements.

For a post angle that would land today, lean into the crowd navigation paper with a punchy take: what does it mean that robots are now learning social behavior from watching humans rather than from explicit programming? That tension between mimicry and understanding is primed for engagement.
