# Community trends (updated 2026-10-22T21:15Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several fronts right now. Tesla's Optimus is generating skepticism, with the capex guidance reversal ($5B up, then $5B down) fueling doubt about real production progress, and Musk's credibility taking repeated hits. Humanoid robotics broadly is a hot debate, with people questioning the logic of bipedal form factors, ethical questions around robot servitude, and Unitree's new "combat robot" variant drawing cautious attention. On the funding side, Dream Labs ($90M seed), Reliable Robotics ($115M), and Eka Robotics ($150M Series A at $1.45B valuation) are driving conversation about where serious money is flowing. NVIDIA's GTC Berlin appearance, spotlighting the DSX Pod and autonomous humanoid robotics for European AI infrastructure, is getting traction. Community-level robotics, from antweight combat bots to Lego teams, shows genuine grassroots enthusiasm.
