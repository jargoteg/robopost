# Community trends (updated 2026-09-28T03:56Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several fronts right now. The wealth-and-poverty framing around AI/robotics is drawing heavy skepticism, with Elon Musk's $370B "solution to poverty" comment generating sharp pushback. Humanoid robots are a dominant thread, split between genuine excitement over milestone demos (half-marathon records, GPT-6 Astra embodiment) and pointed mockery of overhyped or failed reveals, especially Russia's collapsing Aidol and the recurring "guy in a suit" jokes. The Realbotix pivot from sex robots to companion AI is sparking a serious researcher-led thread worth watching. Engineering fundamentals are getting surprising traction, with the Oldham coupling and folded-structure posts performing well, suggesting appetite for "timeless tech" content. Swarm robotics is a quieter but growing undercurrent following the Swarmer-Ratel defense deal.

For a post angle today, lean into the Oldham coupling energy and contrast elegant 200-year-old mechanical solutions with the chaos of overpromised humanoid demos. That tension between timeless engineering and hype cycles is clearly resonating.
