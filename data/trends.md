# Community trends (updated 2026-11-10T01:13Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several fronts right now. Tesla's decision to axe the Cybertruck and double down on Optimus humanoid robots is generating significant discussion, with many questioning whether mass-producing humanoids is realistic. Agility Robotics' Nasdaq IPO as the first pure-play humanoid company is sparking debate about whether people are buying in. There's growing skepticism about humanoid hype more broadly, with Ars Technica's coverage of Agility's own cautious S-4 filing getting traction. On the technical side, Fable 5.2's claimed robotics capabilities and Apple researchers using a Mac mini to control a Unitree humanoid are drawing attention. The Ukraine conflict keeps surfacing through Ratel and Milrem Robotics mentions.

A post angle that would land today: take Agility's own IPO risk disclosures and ask the community directly whether humanoid robots are a 2026 investment or a 2036 investment, framing it as an honest reckoning with the gap between hype and hardware reality.
