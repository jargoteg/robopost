# Community trends (updated 2026-09-23T03:33Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several intersecting conversations right now. The wealth-and-robots discourse is hot, sparked by a billionaire's claim that AI and robotics will solve poverty, drawing significant skepticism. Humanoid robots are getting roasted from multiple angles: Russia's collapsing debut robot, Elon Musk's alleged "guy in a suit" stunt, and a viral Onion piece about a robot beating Usain Bolt are all circulating together as a kind of collective eye-roll at industry hype. Serve Robotics and Unitree are getting specific product attention, with the $2,700 Unitree unit generating genuine alarm about Chinese robotics competition. Engineering heritage content is performing surprisingly well, with the 1821 Oldham coupling and folded-structure biomechanics posts racking up thousands of engagements. Swarm robotics is a quieter but growing thread. The Realbotix pivot thread from a researcher is gaining traction too.

A strong post angle today would be a straight-faced engineering breakdown of what humanoid robots actually cannot do yet, riding the hype-skepticism wave already dominating feeds.
