# Community trends (updated 2026-08-10T01:46Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several overlapping conversations right now. Humanoid robots are drawing the most heat, with deep skepticism about their practicality, a viral clip of a Russian humanoid collapsing at its debut, and ongoing mockery of remote-controlled "autonomous" demos. The billionaire-utopia framing of AI/robotics as a poverty solution is generating significant cynical pushback. Engineering fundamentals are getting unexpected love, with high engagement on the Oldham coupling and folded structures posts suggesting appetite for substance over hype. Robot learning is a quieter but steady thread, including a noted demo of humanoids learning crowd navigation from just five hours of human footage with zero robot training data. Realbotix's pivot from sex robots to classrooms is sparking a longer threaded discussion worth watching.

The post angle most likely to land today: a sharp, evidence-based take on why the most viral humanoid robot moments keep being failures, and what that actually reveals about where the real robotics progress is happening.
