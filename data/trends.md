# Community trends (updated 2026-08-09T18:40Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several fronts right now. Humanoid robots are drawing heavy skepticism, with recurring jokes about demos being remotely controlled by humans, the practicality gap versus industrial robots, and Elon Musk's Optimus credibility problems. A viral mocap-suit clip of a man kicking himself remains a crowd favorite. The billionaire-savior narrative is getting pushback, particularly around claims that AI and robotics will solve poverty. Robot learning is a quiet undercurrent, including a notable paper on humanoid crowd navigation trained purely on human walking data with zero robot demonstrations. Realbotix's pivot from sex robots to classrooms is generating alarmed discussion among researchers. Swarm robotics is present but underserved relative to its engagement potential.
