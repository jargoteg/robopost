# Community trends (updated 2026-10-31T04:20Z)

The dominant conversation this Halloween is the ethics and dual-use tension in robotics: the top post (709 eng) laments the near-impossibility of working on drones without contributing to weapons, echoed by darker sentiments about the industry enabling harm. A related thread covers Trump's decision to pause robotics tariffs while flagging national security concerns around imported hardware. On the technical side, Bear Robotics' KINO-2 humanoid manipulation demo is drawing attention, particularly the inference speed reduction from 500ms to 60ms. The open robotics textbook from UdeM/Mila is getting quiet but genuine traction among practitioners. Cultural conversations are lively too, with Japan's robot-friendly philosophy and the "we just wanted dancing robots" post (613 eng) capturing widespread frustration that the industry drifted toward militarization and surveillance.
