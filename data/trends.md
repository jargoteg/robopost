# Community trends (updated 2026-08-07T13:02Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several fronts right now. Humanoid robots are dominating conversation with heavy skepticism, ranging from mockery of "autonomous" demos secretly controlled by humans, to Russia's robot collapsing on debut, to broader pushback that humanoids reflect a lack of imagination about what robots could be. There's sharp cynicism toward billionaire narratives, particularly a $370B figure claiming AI and robotics will solve poverty. The Realbotix pivot from sex robots to classrooms is generating substantive thread discussion from researchers. Engineering heritage content (Oldham coupling, folded structures) is performing surprisingly well. OpenAI's Pentagon robotics guardrails story is drawing quiet but serious engagement.
