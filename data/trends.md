# Community trends (updated 2026-09-13T03:26Z)

The robotics community is buzzing across several fronts right now. Humanoid robots are dominating conversation, with heavy skepticism about real-world utility, authenticity of demos, and whether the humanoid form factor even makes sense. Russia's collapsing debut robot and the half-marathon record claim are generating particular mockery. Military robotics is drawing attention via the Swarmer/Ratel $224M AI swarm deal, with some unease about Pentagon-AI guardrails surfacing through the OpenAI story. The wealth-and-robotics discourse is flaring up around the billionaire "solve poverty with AI and robots" take, generating pushback. Engineering appreciation content is performing surprisingly well, with the Oldham coupling and folded structures posts both crossing 2,500 engagements. Realbotix's pivot from sex robots to companion AI is generating a dedicated thread from a researcher worth watching. Swarm robotics has two fresh papers circulating quietly.

A strong post angle today would challenge the humanoid form-factor obsession directly, given how primed this audience is to agree that it reflects imagination failure rather than engineering ambition.
